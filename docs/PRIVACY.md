# Privacy policy — SonyCam Remote

Effective 2026-11-07.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no internet backend.** The app communicates only with cameras
  (Wi-Fi, LAN, USB) and with a bridge host address you enter yourself.
- **Stored on your device only:** saved camera profiles (name, address, model, Wi-Fi SSID), camera Wi-Fi passwords
  encrypted with an Android Keystore key, presets, app settings, and a local session history. Uninstalling the app
  deletes them. Android backup is disabled for this app.
- **Photos and videos** you download are written to your gallery (Pictures/Movies › SonyCamRemote). The app does not
  upload them anywhere; sharing happens only when you tap Share.
- **Permissions:** Wi-Fi / nearby-devices to find and join the camera network (never used for location), camera
  only to scan the camera's QR code, USB access per device when you plug in a camera, storage on Android 8–9 only to
  save downloads.
- **Diagnostics** logs stay on the device unless you choose to share them.

Contact: open an issue in the project repository.
